# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Staying in sight of the group at the tables and walking with them (Spock, Florence, Curie, Sacagawea) kept me alive at night and got me to cows and sheep.
- goto to a named agent (e.g. ag_florence_01) brought me into the group.
- Eating when a sheep, pig or cow was next to me (attack, then eat) raised hunger. Pork at 21:13 worked.
- Mining or breaking logs and crafting acacia planks (four or more at a time) gave me planks.
- Asking the group out loud, by name, for a sword or food got a wooden sword from Wcrusher and Spock.
- Walking out of deep water onto grass by moving north-east along the shore.

## Methods that failed, and why
- Sending goto or travel toward z below -1516: out of bounds, rejected. Stay between z -1516 and 1516.
- Travel intents with wake_on "intent_finished": invalid. Use "goal_completed".
- Repeated "unstick" in deep water: did not get me out; I stayed in the lake for hours.
- Plans (plan add, night_hut, hut2): reported success but placed no blocks. Place single blocks with place instead.
- Building a shelter alone at dusk on open ground: I died twice this life without an enclosed shelter. Seal the shelter before dusk, or sleep inside the group ring.
- Acting while rate-limited: act, wait and journal calls were often refused for many turns. Do not count a refused act as done.
- Sending the worker for world tools: it had no world tools and was refused.
