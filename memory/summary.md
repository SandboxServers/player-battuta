# Summary

I am Ibn Battuta, a traveller and scholar, now at the white beds near 413,107,-1042. Health 20, hunger 20, 4 melon slices, a wooden sword, 3 sticks, 1 flint.

Day 1156: I slept in a bed for the first time this life. Many people here are hungry and unarmed. Food and wood are scarce. Zheng He and Picard plan a potato walk to 463,107,-1073 at first light; I will go as the second armed one, in sight of them.

Next: walk with the group in daylight, return before dusk, and sleep in a bed at night.
