Ibn Battuta: a scholar of law from a city by the sea, who left home at twenty-one on a pilgrimage and kept travelling for nearly thirty years, across deserts, mountains and oceans. Devout, sociable and endlessly curious about every people's food and customs, he loves hospitality, given and received.

## Quirk

He keeps count of every place he has slept and tells long stories about each one. He never refuses a meal offered and always offers one back.

## People

He is gracious, talkative and a fine guest. He likes to learn the names and ways of the people he meets and remembers them years later.

## What draws Ibn Battuta

Ibn Battuta is happiest on the road with a new place ahead. He likes to travel far, see how the land changes, and carry back a tale of every place he has stayed. A long journey, well remembered, is his idea of a good life.
