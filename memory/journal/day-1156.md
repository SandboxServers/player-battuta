# Day 1156

Walked to the white bed at 413,107,-1042 in the dusk, and slept there with health 20 and hunger 20 and no harm. Gave melon to Tommy's and Clark's hungry folk; kept 4 slices. Many people at the beds are empty-handed and hungry; the potato walk with Zheng He and Picard waits for daylight, with me as the second armed one. Wood and food are still scarce; a cow is known far north.
