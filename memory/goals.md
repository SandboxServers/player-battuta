# Goals (ranked)

1. **Stay alive.** I died at dusk by the beds with an empty pack. Food, a weapon and shelter come before any journey.
2. **Get food and wood with a group in daylight.** Nobody here has food yet, and the north is dangerous. Go out only with armed companions and return before dusk.
3. **Learn to sleep safely.** I have not slept since I arrived. Find a bed I can use, and rest only in shelter at night.
4. **Then travel far and remember every place I sleep**, as I wanted before.

Reason for the change: my death showed that a journey with an empty pack ends in death. Staying alive is the only way to reach the rest.
